About the data

QR Atlas gives every company a page whose QR code is a small 3D world: a tree or a city growing out of the code. Tap it, or press “I see QR”, and it settles into a flat QR code that scans back to that page, so a post or slide about a company can point to one stable link.

The home page is the same idea at full scale: one large QR code that scans to this site, with the map laid across its tiles and every company standing at its headquarters. Towers grow with revenue; startups are trees, rockets (aerospace and defense) or glowing orbs (AI). The flat map shows the same data in 2D.

Fortune 500

Ranks, names, revenue and profit come from the 2019 Fortune 500 list (fiscal 2018 figures), as published in the open dataset at github.com/cmusam/fortune500. Fortune publishes a new list every June; ranks here are not current.

HQ coordinates come from the HIFLD Fortune 500 Corporate Headquarters dataset (via github.com/anyfactor/f500hqviz), corrected by hand where that file was wrong or out of date. Companies that later moved their headquarters are shown at the new location:

  • McKesson: San Francisco, CA → Irving, TX (2019)
  • Boeing: Chicago, IL → Arlington, VA (2022)
  • RTX: Farmington, CT → Arlington, VA (2020)
  • Caterpillar: Peoria, IL → Irving, TX (2022)
  • Honeywell: Morris Township, NJ → Charlotte, NC (2019)
  • Oracle: Redwood City, CA → Austin, TX (2020)
  • HPE: Palo Alto, CA → Houston, TX (2022)
  • Tesla: Palo Alto, CA → Austin, TX (2021)
  • CBRE Group: Los Angeles, CA → Dallas, TX (2020)
  • AECOM: Los Angeles, CA → Dallas, TX (2021)
  • Charles Schwab: San Francisco, CA → Westlake, TX (2021)

A few companies are shown under the name they use today (for example Facebook as Meta Platforms); their page notes the name on the list.

Startups

The 81 startups and private companies are a hand-picked set of well-known names, not a ranking. Funding and valuations change too fast to keep in a static site, so the pages leave them out.

Map

Borders from Natural Earth via world-atlas and us-atlas. QR codes are generated in your browser with qrcode-generator (MIT).
