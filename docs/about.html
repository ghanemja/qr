    About the data

    QR Compass gives every company a page with its own 3D model. Tap it, or press “Turn into QR code”, and the model breaks into pixels that settle into a QR code that scans back to that page, so a post or slide about a company can point to one stable link.

    The home page is a 3D map of the US in seven regions. Each region’s biggest companies stand on it near their headquarters; click a region to break it off the map and explore every company in it.

    Fortune 500

    Ranks, names, revenue, profit, industry and headquarters city come from the 2026 Fortune 500 list (fiscal 2025 figures) as published at fortune.com. Fortune publishes a new list every June.

    Headquarters are placed at their city using the US Census Bureau’s 2024 Gazetteer of places. Some companies appear under a short name (for example IBM, BNY, USAA); their page notes the name on the list.

    Startups

    The 59 startups and private companies are a hand-picked set of well-known names, not a ranking. Funding and valuations change too fast to keep in a static site, so the pages leave them out.

    Map

    Borders from Natural Earth via world-atlas and us-atlas. QR codes are generated in your browser with qrcode-generator (MIT).

    3D models

    Every model on the site can be downloaded from the 3D asset library as a GLB file, ready for Blender, game engines and 3D viewers. Each file carries the project credit and the full licence text.

    Corrections

    Edit data/fortune500.json or data/startups.json and run npm run build.
